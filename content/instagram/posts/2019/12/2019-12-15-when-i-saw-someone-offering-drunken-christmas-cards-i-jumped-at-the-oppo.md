# When I saw someone offering drunken Christmas cards I jumped at…

December 15, 2019 · 10:55 AM UTC

When I saw someone offering drunken Christmas cards I jumped at the opportunity and asked them to draw me an elephant. I thought this would be more drunk and less like a really fulfilling relationship that reminds me that my inability to open up emotionally is going to preclude me from ever finding that kind of happiness.
