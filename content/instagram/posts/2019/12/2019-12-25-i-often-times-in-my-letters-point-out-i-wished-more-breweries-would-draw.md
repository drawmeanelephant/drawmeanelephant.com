# I often times in my letters point out I wished more breweries…

December 25, 2019 · 5:21 PM UTC

I often times in my letters point out I wished more breweries would draw me lower quality elephants more frequently rather than breweries drawing me really high quality stuff.
These are 10 of the best elephants that I often describe as better than I deserve and I cannot describe the feeling of being the monkey behind a typewriter who ends up with Shakespeare opening these things in my mail.
Elephants from [@galusaurus](https://www.instagram.com/galusaurus/) via [@thewaystation](https://www.instagram.com/thewaystation/) [@dogfishhead](https://www.instagram.com/dogfishhead/) [@geneseebrewery](https://www.instagram.com/geneseebrewery/) [@that_craig_guy_13](https://www.instagram.com/that_craig_guy_13/) via [@newenglandbrewing](https://www.instagram.com/newenglandbrewing/) [@bearpawriverbrew](https://www.instagram.com/bearpawriverbrew/) [@mountaintoad](https://www.instagram.com/mountaintoad/) [@rhoxrose](https://www.instagram.com/rhoxrose/) via [@dryriverbrewing](https://www.instagram.com/dryriverbrewing/)  and I can't begin to thank these places enough. <a href="../../../tags/drawmeanelephant.html">#drawmeanelephant</a>
