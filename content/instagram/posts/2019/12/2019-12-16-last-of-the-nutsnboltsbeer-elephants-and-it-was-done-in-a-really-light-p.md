# Last of the @nutsnboltsbeer elephants and it was done in a really…

December 16, 2019 · 7:19 AM UTC

Last of the [@nutsnboltsbeer](https://www.instagram.com/nutsnboltsbeer/) elephants and it was done in a really light pencil. Just going to throw out that [@apsarapencil_](https://www.instagram.com/apsarapencil_/) Absolute is probably cheaper an upgrade over whatever pencil they used and scans in much easier. They are like $6 on Prime and come with a sharpener and an eraser \(which I don't use as that means admitting my mistakes, another big reason contributing to me not being able to maintain a committed relationship\). I put a note in the letters to discourage the use of 2HB, HB, or other suckass pencils. <a href="../../../tags/drawmeanelephant.html">#drawmeanelephant</a>
