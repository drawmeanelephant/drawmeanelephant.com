# @sebagobrewing in Kennebunk sent just a coaster which seemed weird…

September 9, 2019 · 10:39 AM UTC

[@sebagobrewing](https://www.instagram.com/sebagobrewing/) in Kennebunk sent just a coaster which seemed weird but then I remembered that my dad always said “son, there’s two sides to every coaster”. His words are as true today as when he was alive and they did an excellent elephant on the other side.
This is a true stroke of genius turning the logo and coaster into an elephant and I’m pretty sure when the idea came to their head there were numbers and mathematical symbols flying around in LS3D on some Goodwill Hunting genius. I realize that’s probably Good Will Hunting but I think the upcycled nature makes my way accidentally better. It could be the title of the movie as Goodwill too but I’m going to leave that as a Shrödinger's Cat kind of thing and not Google it. <a href="../../../tags/drawmeanelephant.html">#drawmeanelephant</a>
