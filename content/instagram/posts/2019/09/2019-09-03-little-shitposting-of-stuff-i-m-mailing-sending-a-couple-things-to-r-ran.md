September 3, 2019 · 8:55 AM UTC

Little <a href="../../../tags/shitposting.html">#shitposting</a> of stuff I'm mailing sending a couple things to [@r.randomactsofcards](https://www.instagram.com/r.randomactsofcards/) people including this gem to someone in Utah. <a href="../../../tags/walloftext.html">#walloftext</a> below

Make this long enough so no one reads it? Sure\! Also working on the backer to go back to [@officedepot](https://www.instagram.com/officedepot/) Stellar paper which I just like a lot better than [@clairefontaine_officiel](https://www.instagram.com/clairefontaine_officiel/) and especially so since it's a tenth of the cost, more surface area, lighter, and I don't have to deal with pen shops that seem to all bring out the worst in me.
