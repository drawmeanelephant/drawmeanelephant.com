# @eveysrainydays1 drew this damned fine elephant for a friend of…

September 24, 2019 · 2:11 PM UTC

[@eveysrainydays1](https://www.instagram.com/eveysrainydays1/) drew this damned fine elephant for a friend of mine as a commission. I think he spends less per elephant on average if you use a truncated mean than I do. You don’t notice the spending when you buy stamps at the post office like I do rather than commission the art out of beer money like he does. You can get a lot of [@pabstblueribbon](https://www.instagram.com/pabstblueribbon/) for what it costs to get a single elephant.
All that aside I think we’ve got a young female Asian elephant. I don’t know much about elephants but the smaller eared ones are Asian, I’ve heard but not verified. The tusks look small which is why I guess younger, but again not in any way sure how tusks even work. I guess female because the purple inked ones are obviously girls, while blue or red are boys. <a href="../../../tags/drawmeanelephant.html">#drawmeanelephant</a>
