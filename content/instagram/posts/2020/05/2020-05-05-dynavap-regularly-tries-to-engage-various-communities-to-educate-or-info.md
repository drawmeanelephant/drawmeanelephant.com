# @dynavap regularly tries to engage various communities to educate…

May 5, 2020 · 6:52 AM UTC

[@dynavap](https://www.instagram.com/dynavap/) regularly tries to engage various communities to educate or inform on new products or how to use them. As much as I respect that, like every brewery just gets randomly asked when they are bringing back some beer from five years ago I came out of left field and asked them to <a href="../../../tags/drawmeanelephant.html">#drawmeanelephant</a> and they not only did, but also 3D printed an elephant <a href="../../../tags/vapcap.html">#vapcap</a> holder that somehow attracts the metal to the elephant <a href="../../../tags/howdomagnetswork.html">#howdomagnetswork</a>
Also, they earned a spot on my keyring with [@dogfishhead](https://www.instagram.com/dogfishhead/) and someone I don't know their Instagram handle. These photos incidentally have about 3/4 of my swag, which isn't a bad haul for thirty bills of postage. It is a ridiculously nice keychain and their stationery game is on the next level. Also, not that anyone reads this far but I didn't get a free <a href="../../../tags/2020m.html">#2020M</a> as that was a [@puffitup](https://www.instagram.com/puffitup/) pre\-order which came with another elephant and a <a href="../../../tags/karmavap.html">#karmavap</a> that [@psychictshirt](https://www.instagram.com/psychictshirt/) promised me an elephant for. For as much as it costs to get one brewery elephant in postage this is like getting three elephants with a free vape, though.
