# After @trafficjamdetroit drew elephants I got motivated to finish…

February 1, 2020 · 9:18 AM UTC

After [@trafficjamdetroit](https://www.instagram.com/trafficjamdetroit/) drew elephants I got motivated to finish a version of this that is just printed. [@endlesspens](https://www.instagram.com/endlesspens/) doesn't need to worry as I ordered more fountain pen stuff from them yesterday, but I realistically am not going to be able to write 6,500\+ more breweries by hand as I wrote the last 1,300\+.
