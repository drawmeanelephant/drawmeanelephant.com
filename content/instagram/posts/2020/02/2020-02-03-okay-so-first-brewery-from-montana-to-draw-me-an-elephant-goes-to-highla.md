# Okay so first brewery from Montana to draw me an elephant goes to…

February 3, 2020 · 11:42 AM UTC

Okay so first brewery from Montana to draw me an elephant goes to [@highlanderbeer](https://www.instagram.com/highlanderbeer/) and it's a really good one. Everyone knows elephants are really strong but the head breweries, Gary, knew that the true measurement of elephant strength is the Trunk Strength Index Ration, or TSIR for short. This is one of the strongest elephants I've seen judging by the thinness of the trunk and knowing a glass picture weighs 83 ounces full. Great work capturing this majestically strong creature\! <a href="../../../tags/drawmeanelephant.html">#drawmeanelephant</a>
