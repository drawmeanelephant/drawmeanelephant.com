# I provide a page with all my letters for people to draw me an…

July 19, 2020 · 1:53 PM UTC

I provide a page with all my letters for people to draw me an elephant but strongly encourage providing your own paper. That does not work at all and most of my elephants are on provided paper. I try to provide the crappiest looking paper possible, but it's actually really nice [@officedepot](https://www.instagram.com/officedepot/) Stellar paper from Vietnam that can take more abuse than any ruled paper without going into those expensive engineering tablets.
