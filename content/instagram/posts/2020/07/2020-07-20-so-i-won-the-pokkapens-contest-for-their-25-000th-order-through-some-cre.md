# So I won the @pokkapens contest for their 25,000th order through…

July 20, 2020 · 7:27 AM UTC

So I won the [@pokkapens](https://www.instagram.com/pokkapens/) contest for their 25,000th order through some creative use of store mechanics. Some would call it an exploit but either way I committed myself to winning before the prize was even announced. The prize turned out to not be any of the stuff I wanted and instead was 100 custom pens. Since I didn’t have any use for the pens I had three ideas and ended up going with <a href="../../../tags/drawmeanelephant.html">#drawmeanelephant</a> instead of some other ideas I won’t share as they are not related to draw me an elephant.

The pens turned out really good and the custom ones are better positioned than the [@riteintherain](https://www.instagram.com/riteintherain/) ones that have the logo all over the place and too close to the <a href="../../../tags/pokkapens.html">#PokkaPens</a> logo. I had a long paragraph about the only thing I saw as a huge mistake and I’m pretty sure no one will notice as it’s practically impossible to see if you don’t have experience in the printing industry. <a href="../../../tags/pickinggnatshitoutofpepper.html">#pickinggnatshitoutofpepper</a> <a href="../../../tags/metashitposting.html">#metashitposting</a> <a href="../../../tags/contest.html">#contest</a> <a href="../../../tags/winner.html">#winner</a> <a href="../../../tags/custompens.html">#custompens</a> <a href="../../../tags/nocluehowhashtagsworkandatthispointimtoaffraidtoask.html">#nocluehowhashtagsworkandatthispointimtoaffraidtoask</a>
