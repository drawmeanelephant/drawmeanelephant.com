March 17, 2020 · 4:33 PM UTC

[@coalcreekcoffee](https://www.instagram.com/coalcreekcoffee/) or [@coalcreektap](https://www.instagram.com/coalcreektap/) if you prefer drew this one. One of their people got ahold of me and sent like a concept and said they were rusty at drawing. NGL I didn’t have super high hopes but this is really good for just intriguing or whatever. Like the stare keeps you from noticing anything else but the coffee. Pretty heady and was on what I think was <a href="../../../tags/tomoeriverpaper.html">#tomoeriverpaper</a> or similarly nice 50\# or lighter.
