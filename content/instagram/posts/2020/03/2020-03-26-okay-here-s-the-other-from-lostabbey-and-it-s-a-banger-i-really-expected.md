# Okay here's the other from @lostabbey and it's a banger.

March 26, 2020 · 10:07 AM UTC

Okay here's the other from [@lostabbey](https://www.instagram.com/lostabbey/) and it's a banger. I really expected in the last 4 months to see a lot of repeats but they have stayed really original somehow. Never ceases to amaze me. Stickers that came with also here and the Tiny Bubbles reminded me that my mother used to tell me the story of her best friend losing her virginity to Don Ho back ages ago when that kind of behavior was still acceptable. Thanks parents for ruining everything and thanks [@lostabbey](https://www.instagram.com/lostabbey/) for participating.
