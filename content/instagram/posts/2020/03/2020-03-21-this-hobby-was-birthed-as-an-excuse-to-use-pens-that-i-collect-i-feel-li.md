# This hobby was birthed as an excuse to use pens that I collect.

March 21, 2020 · 8:12 AM UTC

This hobby was birthed as an excuse to use pens that I collect. I feel like my pen collection is pretty complete unless I can get [@woodyharrelson](https://www.instagram.com/woodyharrelson/) to pimp me out to Robert Redford but there are oddities I still pick up. I recently got an ancient [@carandache.official](https://www.instagram.com/carandache.official/) <a href="../../../tags/849.html">#849</a> to to with my others as well as my <a href="../../../tags/858.html">#858</a> and <a href="../../../tags/ecridor.html">#ecridor</a>

This is the pen you want in your pocket or your checkbook. [@endlesspens](https://www.instagram.com/endlesspens/) sells the Nespresso one I have but the rest are wayyyy too old for them. They drew me an elephant with the <a href="../../../tags/nespresso849.html">#nespresso849</a> on this feed somewhere.
