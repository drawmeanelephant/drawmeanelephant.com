# Okay so February was huge.

March 4, 2020 · 9:18 AM UTC

Okay so February was huge. I sent about 800 letters out in short order and they are still rolling in. I haven't ran math to see how bad I faired but I'm feeling it my gut each elephant will cost me about $14 in hard costs and I shutter to imagine time.
Regardless of how stupid this project may be I'm writing another 3,500 and going to complete the entire list. Probably before spring is over I'll have written 95% breweries in the United States to ask them to draw me an elephant.
Bonus photo is some of the dozens of handwritten letters that never made it. I don't speak good millennial or younger but I'd probably say <a href="../../../tags/bigoof.html">#bigoof</a> or maybe <a href="../../../tags/tag.html">#ｂｉｇｏｏｆ</a>
