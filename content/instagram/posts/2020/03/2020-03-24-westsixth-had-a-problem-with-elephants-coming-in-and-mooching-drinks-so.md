# @westsixth had a problem with elephants coming in and mooching…

March 24, 2020 · 6:51 AM UTC

[@westsixth](https://www.instagram.com/westsixth/) had a problem with elephants coming in and mooching drinks so they require each elephant perform two tricks if they want a free beer. This <a href="../../../tags/thorstyboi.html">#thorstyboi</a> is not fiddlefarting around and is pulling both tricks at once. Thanks [@barfhaus](https://www.instagram.com/barfhaus/) for illustrating how West Sixth handles it, sending the stickers, and knowing how to fold a letter.
