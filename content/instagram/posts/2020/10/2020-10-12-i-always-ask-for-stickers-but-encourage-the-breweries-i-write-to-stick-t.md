# I always ask for stickers, but encourage the I write to stick them…

October 12, 2020 · 4:28 AM UTC

I always ask for stickers, but encourage the <a href="../../../tags/breweries.html">#breweries</a> I write to stick them on their drawing for <a href="../../../tags/lazybranding.html">#lazybranding</a> and [@eternitybrewing](https://www.instagram.com/eternitybrewing/) are good readers\! They did a great job with the elephant, but had no space when it was finished for the sticker other than over this elephant’s face. It’s a good thing as the elephant was making a really silly face. <a href="../../../tags/eternitybrewing.html">#eternitybrewing</a> is a <a href="../../../tags/michiganbrewery.html">#michiganbrewery</a> and joins [@bellsbrewery](https://www.instagram.com/bellsbrewery/) [@trafficjamdetroit](https://www.instagram.com/trafficjamdetroit/) and more from <a href="../../../tags/michigan.html">#michigan</a> who actually answered when I asked them to <a href="../../../tags/drawmeanelephant.html">#drawmeanelephant</a>
