# Jeff put together a really fantastic elephant with what appears to…

December 23, 2020 · 9:30 AM UTC

Jeff put together a really fantastic <a href="../../../tags/postitart.html">#postitart</a> elephant with what appears to be a [@sharpie](https://www.instagram.com/sharpie/) Ultra Fine Point that replaced the Extra Fine Points that were actually good.
