# Alex of @puffitup just sent in an unmerciful assault of dad jokes…

December 25, 2020 · 7:19 AM UTC

Alex of [@puffitup](https://www.instagram.com/puffitup/) just sent in an unmerciful assault of dad jokes or puns or whatever this homophone is. I didn't grow up with a wholesome enough family to appreciate these as my first jokes were bar jokes and other problematic 80s jokes about Nancy Reagan losing a tit and Michael Jackson's hair catching on fire. I first heard this joke in the early 90s in 5th grade from our teacher and when no one laughed you could watch a little of her die on the inside. It's a really good elephant but I didn't leave much room to talk about it.
