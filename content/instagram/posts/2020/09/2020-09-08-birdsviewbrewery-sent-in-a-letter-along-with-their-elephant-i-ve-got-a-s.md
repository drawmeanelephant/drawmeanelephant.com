# @birdsviewbrewery sent in a letter along with their elephant.

September 8, 2020 · 8:26 AM UTC

[@birdsviewbrewery](https://www.instagram.com/birdsviewbrewery/) sent in a letter along with their elephant. I’ve got a serious love for ink stamps and they got everything right here somehow and I very rarely say that when I get anything <a href="../../../tags/prioritymail.html">#prioritymail</a> as that usually means they don’t know how to mail stuff. Not the case here. This thing is huuuuuuuuge and the priority wasn’t just some <a href="../../../tags/proudpapa.html">#proudpapa</a> moment where they are overprotecting some lame drawing and I need to bite my and resist the urge to show the world that my mom taught me how to be unappreciative. This was the best way to ship and contained only the letter, drawing, stickers, and some boards from <a href="../../../tags/amazonpackaging.html">#amazonpackaging</a> to secure the whole thing. This was absolutely masterful and will probably be the best I get out of the 1900 letters I’ve recently sent. Super nice art and not super proud of it with any overpacking.
