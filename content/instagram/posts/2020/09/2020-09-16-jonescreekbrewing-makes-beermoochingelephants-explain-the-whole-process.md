# @jonescreekbrewing makes explain the whole process of brewing beer…

September 16, 2020 · 8:17 AM UTC

[@jonescreekbrewing](https://www.instagram.com/jonescreekbrewing/) makes <a href="../../../tags/beermoochingelephants.html">#beermoochingelephants</a> explain the whole process of brewing beer in order to get free drinks. Elephants are naturally quite adept at explaining saccharide chains \(and really more over any carbohydrate\), so they added some geometry.
