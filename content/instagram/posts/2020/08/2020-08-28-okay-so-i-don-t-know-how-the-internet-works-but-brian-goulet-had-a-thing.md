# Okay so I don't know how the internet works but @brian.goulet had…

August 28, 2020 · 9:02 AM UTC

Okay so I don't know how the internet works but [@brian.goulet](https://www.instagram.com/brian.goulet/) had a thing on [@youtube](https://www.instagram.com/youtube/) and it was for [@gouletpens](https://www.instagram.com/gouletpens/) for the <a href="../../../tags/noodlerscharliepen.html">#noodlerscharliepen</a> which I love and own a lot of. [@penaddict](https://www.instagram.com/penaddict/) follows me and does actual review stuff, so follow him for something serious. I wrote some words in the Charlie and HOLY HELL HERE'S A LOT OF INDIAN PENS SO BUCKLE UP. After the Charlies are two Kanwrite Apex with [@conway_stewart](https://www.instagram.com/conway_stewart/) nibs in them and yes two 70¢ pens with these nibs are probably my favorite pens. Next are [@noodlersink](https://www.instagram.com/noodlersink/) <a href="../../../tags/ahab.html">#ahab</a> pens followed by [@kanwrite_kanpurwriters](https://www.instagram.com/kanwrite_kanpurwriters/) Heritage \(piston filler, sorry the demo is full of [@jherbininks](https://www.instagram.com/jherbininks/) Emeril da Chauffeur\) and some more Kanwrite. Another Kanwrite with a [@pelikan_usa](https://www.instagram.com/pelikan_usa/) nib from a mid 1990s trainwreck that all the sections rotted and no other pen is compatible from Pelikan. Rounded off with some [@asapens](https://www.instagram.com/asapens/) and a [@ranga_pens](https://www.instagram.com/ranga_pens/) mixed in. This is a ton of Indian kit and these are all great pens, even if they look odd. Friction fit Indian made feeds.
