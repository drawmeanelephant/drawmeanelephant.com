# If you want fountain pens I'd imagine pretty much everyone just…

August 28, 2020 · 9:40 AM UTC

If you want fountain pens I'd imagine pretty much everyone just shops local as that would make sense. I have some here that are from Ohio and it's kind of your instinct a little bit to be proud of your home state. I'm sure every state has a few of these guys, but [@edisonpenco](https://www.instagram.com/edisonpenco/) makes some ridiculously nice pens and is out of northern Ohio while Bexley is out of Bexley and just kind of antisocial and I don't think does social media. I don't know if everyone in the pen world loves Howard Levy but any time I hear him mentioned someone pipes up that he's their favorite person at pen shows. Definitely one of mine. Couple of cheaper Bexley Prometheus, a Submariner, a Molteni Curakova contract pen, an Edison Menlo Pump Filler, and an Extended Mina.
