# I gave away all my pens I won to people in their @pokkapens…

August 11, 2020 · 9:22 AM UTC

I gave away all my pens I won to people in their [@pokkapens](https://www.instagram.com/pokkapens/)  Facebook group in exchange for elephants. Everyone said they weren’t very good at art but would try their best. This is a totally valid move drawing someone else’s elephant and considering they also mailed stamps the whole thing cost me $1.05. It is basically two free elephants. You can’t just expect people to come up with two of their own original elephants for three pens. If you find that source of creative talent I’ll gladly reorder more pens.
