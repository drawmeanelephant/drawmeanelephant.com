# Okay so we established pretty early into this hobby that I'm the…

August 23, 2020 · 8:26 AM UTC

Okay so we established pretty early into this hobby that I'm the only one really crazy about pens. That hasn't stopped almost all my handwritten letters from mostly being about pens and ink, rather than beer or elephants which would make sense as I'm writing breweries to draw me an elephant. I try to keep my selfawareness pretty low and back to pens, here's the ones that are maybe passable priced low to high \(high on top\). The <a href="../../../tags/300rt.html">#300RT</a> is ultimately a cheap ballpoint but has the "good pen" feel at low price. When cleaning out your pen drawer this pen would get spared. The <a href="../../../tags/papermateprofile.html">#papermateprofile</a> has a really lousy feel but it's unmistakably like a <a href="../../../tags/pilotg2.html">#pilotg2</a> and a lot people like that. I hate them like super passionate because it's Pilot and while this one is charming for being not Pilot I always hate it like that song <a href="../../../tags/lastkiss.html">#lastkiss</a> that Pearl Jam does. Not that I don't think the PJ version is better, just an awful song. Anyways, <a href="../../../tags/sharpiesgel.html">#sharpiesgel</a> is like 8¢ more than the shitty Profile so that's a no brainer and <a href="../../../tags/pokkapens.html">#pokkapens</a> are more still. That brings me to the <a href="../../../tags/inkjoygelpens.html">#inkjoygelpens</a> that I want in Grey as my like ideal goal. As I said those absolutely don't exist as a single pen option. It's gonna be a big stretch that [@paper_mate](https://www.instagram.com/paper_mate/) or [@newellbrands](https://www.instagram.com/newellbrands/) will mess with it, but I have an [@aaronrodgers12](https://www.instagram.com/aaronrodgers12/) <a href="../../../tags/sharpie.html">#sharpie</a> and they made a custom one for another dude but I don't like to talk about it. Last we have a <a href="../../../tags/stackedcelluloid.html">#stackedcelluloid</a> Italian made [@lamy_official](https://www.instagram.com/lamy_official/) from like WW2 era. I'd never trade this for an elephant as you'll never see one like it, but I also wanted to just include a pen you'll never be able to own. Cheers and happy Sunday\! <a href="../../../tags/pens.html">#pens</a> <a href="../../../tags/shitpost.html">#shitpost</a>
