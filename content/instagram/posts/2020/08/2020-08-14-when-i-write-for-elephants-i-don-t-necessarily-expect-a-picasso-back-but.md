# When I write for elephants I don’t necessarily expect a back, but…

August 14, 2020 · 2:44 PM UTC

When I write for elephants I don’t necessarily expect a <a href="../../../tags/picasso.html">#picasso</a> back, but it’s totally cool when they do\! Jon of [@portcitybrew](https://www.instagram.com/portcitybrew/) had the task of drawing and produced a replica of a Picasso. Perhaps not like 100% the same value but functionally I’d say it’s 95% or better. They included an additional elephant I’ll post later as well as a poem on the reverse side of this piece, which I’m hoping doesn’t slaughter this thing’s value when I take it to [@roadshowpbs](https://www.instagram.com/roadshowpbs/) later down the road. The whole response from <a href="../../../tags/portcitybrewery.html">#portcitybrewery</a> just dripped with class.
