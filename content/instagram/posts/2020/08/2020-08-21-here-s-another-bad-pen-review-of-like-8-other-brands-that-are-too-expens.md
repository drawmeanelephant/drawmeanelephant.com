# Here's another bad pen review of like 8 other brands that are too…

August 21, 2020 · 12:47 PM UTC

Here's another bad pen review of like 8 other brands that are too expensive or impractical for promo. Half or more of those you shouldn't buy and maybe 3 everyone should own in the form of the the <a href="../../../tags/drgrip.html">#drgrip</a> that I meant to do one for but put away because it looks just like the G2 Limited, and honestly the two are about interchangeable. Anywho, I don't really care for disposables but I felt bad shilling a bunch of [@paper_mate](https://www.instagram.com/paper_mate/) stuff when there's not that much of their kit in my collection. Don't want to come off as a Papermate fanboy or shill. They just sent me pens. <a href="../../../tags/guywholikespens.html">#guywholikespens</a>
