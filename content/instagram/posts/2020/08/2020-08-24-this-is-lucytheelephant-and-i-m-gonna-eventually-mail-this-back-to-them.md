# This is @lucytheelephant and I’m gonna eventually mail this back…

August 24, 2020 · 1:28 PM UTC

This is [@lucytheelephant](https://www.instagram.com/lucytheelephant/) and I’m gonna eventually mail this back to them but keep forgetting. This is old. There’s really no way to know how old, but since <a href="../../../tags/lucytheelephant.html">#lucytheelephant</a> was built in 1881 by the powers of my math I’d say it’s younger than 139 years as the words on the postcard talk about it being there. I was really scratching my head so I looked it up and it has to be older than 1952 on account of the <a href="../../../tags/1centstamp.html">#1centstamp</a> but can’t be 19717–1918 because as everyone knows <a href="../../../tags/ww1.html">#WW1</a> briefly had <a href="../../../tags/2centstamps.html">#2centstamps</a> but really there’s no way one can find out beyond that. Maybe when I send it to Lucy she’ll know on account of the dates they sold postcards. <a href="../../../tags/vintagepostcards.html">#vintagepostcards</a>
