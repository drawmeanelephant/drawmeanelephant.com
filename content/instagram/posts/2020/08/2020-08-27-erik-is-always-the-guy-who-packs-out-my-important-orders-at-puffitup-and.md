# Erik is always the guy who packs out my important orders at…

August 27, 2020 · 11:56 AM UTC

Erik is always the guy who packs out my important orders at [@puffitup](https://www.instagram.com/puffitup/) and is absolutely awesome at packing stuff. He’s not, however, a first string elephant artist. I once requested Erik do something other than a smiley and he did the <a href="../../../tags/treesgrinder.html">#treesgrinder</a> logo \(not from memory either, I had one in my order\). I think this is my first elephant from Erik and I have a stack of cards from him. Great try. <a href="../../../tags/drawmeanelephant.html">#drawmeanelephant</a>
