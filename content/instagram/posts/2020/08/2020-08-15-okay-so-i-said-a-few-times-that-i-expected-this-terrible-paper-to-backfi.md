# Okay so I said a few times that I expected this terrible paper to…

August 15, 2020 · 9:42 AM UTC

Okay so I said a few times that I expected this terrible paper to backfire on me and get crappy elephants. So far been completely the opposite and this little cutie isn’t going to ruin the streak. [@stelmobrewing](https://www.instagram.com/stelmobrewing/) did this one and included a sticker, which is always nice as I have a dedicated <a href="../../../tags/stickerbook.html">#stickerbook</a> for the hobby. I think this elephant was supposed to have a body but the artist didn’t put one on for lack of space. Sometimes it’s better to do less and you’ve only got one shot with this paper, so better to not screw it up. <a href="../../../tags/drawmeanelephant.html">#drawmeanelephant</a> <a href="../../../tags/stelmobrewing.html">#stelmobrewing</a>
