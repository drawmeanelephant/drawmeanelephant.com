# Every time I read the word “indeed” I feel five points smarter and…

August 10, 2020 · 1:02 PM UTC

Every time I read the word “indeed” I feel five points smarter and seven points cheekier. [@indeedbrewing](https://www.instagram.com/indeedbrewing/) covered every base imaginable here to make this this adorable <a href="../../../tags/pinkelephant.html">#pinkelephant</a> presenting a flower welcome, including an <a href="../../../tags/indeedbrewing.html">#indeedbrewing</a> gift tag, coasters, and two stickers. One of the stickers features a beer they brewed that has elephants water skiing on their label, which I have never seen on a beer label until today. Huge thanks to Marley Moon for the artwork. <a href="../../../tags/drawmeanelephant.html">#drawmeanelephant</a> <a href="../../../tags/peachbumipa.html">#peachbumipa</a> <a href="../../../tags/indeedminneapolis.html">#indeedminneapolis</a>
