# @madheaters does what Dynavap hasn’t done with several things, but…

June 28, 2021 · 7:25 AM UTC

[@madheaters](https://www.instagram.com/madheaters/) does what Dynavap hasn’t done with several things, but most importantly this elephant with the <a href="../../../tags/vapcap.html">#vapcap</a> on its trunk. I suppose some of their other inventions such as the temperature sensing induction heater or ridiculously nice stem with novel cooling system are more commercially viable than the elephant drawings, but this is my focus. Their magnets are super strong to the point if I put it any closer to the elephant it would pull the cap right off his trunk and ruin his hit. Last thing I wanna do is blow this stampy’s high.
