# So @jscheuerman did an elephant for @fisherbrewing that was…

April 7, 2021 · 12:24 PM UTC

So [@jscheuerman](https://www.instagram.com/jscheuerman/) did an elephant for [@fisherbrewing](https://www.instagram.com/fisherbrewing/) that was awesome and I sent him some [@pokkapens](https://www.instagram.com/pokkapens/) pens to several months back. Of the 39 people I sent pens to and asked only for an elephant this is the best reply of the bunch. It's not even really close but I may post a couple of the runners up. I included how I've used Josh's elephant in the past and it's a good thing I don't pay royalties on my elephants because the <a href="../../../tags/fisherbrewing.html">#fisherbrewing</a> elephant is royalAF. I'm already long on this write\-up and this is I think my first mammoth if you're keeping track of the various kinds of stampies. Check out Josh's Instagram. He's one of my favorite people I've ran into in this hobby and I've told a lot of people when calling them to thank them for their elephants about what a great guy Josh is, which is probably an asshole thing on my part, but I'm not actually thanking people either. I never say please, thank you, I'm sorry, you're welcome, or any of that stuff. <a href="../../../tags/drawmeanelephant.html">#drawmeanelephant</a> <a href="../../../tags/misterrogerswasright.html">#misterrogerswasright</a> <a href="../../../tags/nocluehowhashtagsworkandatthispointimtoaffraidtoask.html">#nocluehowhashtagsworkandatthispointimtoaffraidtoask</a>
