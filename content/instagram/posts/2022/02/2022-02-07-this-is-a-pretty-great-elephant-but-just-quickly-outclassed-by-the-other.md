# This is a pretty great elephant, but just quickly outclassed by…

February 7, 2022 · 10:11 AM UTC

This is a pretty great elephant, but just quickly outclassed by the other <a href="../../../tags/sharpieart.html">#sharpieart</a> elephant due to lack of toes. It’s easy to go overboard on toes as we’ve seen with some of the seven toed elephants, but it is prudent to put three toes up and if pressed say “the rest are on the other side”. If pressed about how many that would be you just ghost them rather than being the guy googling how many toes an elephant has. <a href="../../../tags/drawmeanelephant.html">#drawmeanelephant</a>
