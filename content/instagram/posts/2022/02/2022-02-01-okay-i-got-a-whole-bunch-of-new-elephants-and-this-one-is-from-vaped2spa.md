# Okay I got a whole bunch of new elephants and this one is from…

February 1, 2022 · 7:57 AM UTC

Okay I got a whole bunch of new elephants and this one is from [@vaped2space](https://www.instagram.com/vaped2space/) who is a super big [@simrellcollection](https://www.instagram.com/simrellcollection/) friend. Every email from <a href="../../../tags/simrellcollection.html">#simrellcollection</a> calls you a friend, so I think that's the proper term. There's a lot more but V2S's elephant was low hanging fruit.
