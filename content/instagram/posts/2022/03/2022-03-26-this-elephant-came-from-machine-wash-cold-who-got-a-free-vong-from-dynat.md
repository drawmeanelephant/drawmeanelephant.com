# This elephant came from @machine.wash.cold who got a free from…

March 26, 2022 · 10:04 AM UTC

This elephant came from [@machine.wash.cold](https://www.instagram.com/machine.wash.cold/) who got a free <a href="../../../tags/vong.html">#VonG</a> from [@dynatography](https://www.instagram.com/dynatography/) which had a midsection break. I replaced their midsection with one from a free <a href="../../../tags/vong.html">#VonG</a> that I got from Will of [@dynavap](https://www.instagram.com/dynavap/) fame along with some pencils. Six months later, some [@carandache](https://www.instagram.com/carandache/) <a href="../../../tags/supracolorsoft.html">#supracolorsoft</a> and a drawing of an elephant shows. Great pencil and nice way for me to give away my old [@sheafferpen](https://www.instagram.com/sheafferpen/) lead from the [@textron](https://www.instagram.com/textron/) days. <a href="../../../tags/drawmeanelephant.html">#drawmeanelephant</a>
