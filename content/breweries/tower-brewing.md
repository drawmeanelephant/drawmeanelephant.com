#### California Rules

I sent a shitton of letters to California and they did really good on delivering with the elephants. I think I wrote pretty much all of them with $2 Chinese pens with [Fude](https://janinescribbles.com/tag/fude-nib/) nibs. Not sure if I had a good attitude or what, but they seemed to be slightly more frequent. I really haven't found any connection between letters that have high effort and getting some sort of a response.
