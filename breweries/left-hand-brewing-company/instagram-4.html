Caption / Correspondence Log:

So this showed up today and since they don't have social media I don't think that's even easier. I do like all this stuff. The stamp makes me think of @jakeandamirofficial #wherethewildthingsarent but anyways there's a few elephants and a @pokkapens with the ass chopped off of it and surgerized onto a cap to make a two assed #pokkapens for those who want more asses on their pen without going full Mephesto for five asses. They included two #lefthandednotebook which I assume is gonna be a pretty weak hashtag. This Sanford ink bottle is super duper old. Oh yeah the elephant appears to be blowing a raspberry out and taunting this alligator or perhaps crocodile. I don't usually try to do much fact check on these, but at a cursory glance this would be the kind of thing that seems implausible to me to occur in nature. I'm pretty weak on my biology and the crocodile seems pretty well drawn so I'm gonna give them the benefit of the doubt. #drawmeanelephant
